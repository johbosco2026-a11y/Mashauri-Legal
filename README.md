# Mashauri Legal

A Kenya-focused legal information and consultation website with practical front-end tools.

## Included

- Issue triage section for employment, property, business, and family matters.
- General notice-period planner based on common Employment Act section 35 categories.
- Indicative land stamp-duty estimator using common 4% urban / 2% rural rates.
- Limitation-date planner for common contract, tort, and land claim categories.
- First-consultation document checklist generator.
- Links to Kenya Law, Judiciary e-filing, and the National Legal Aid Service.
- Prominent legal-information disclaimer and no-relationship notice.

## Important

This is a static front-end website. The contact form displays a client-side success message and does not transmit or store submissions. Calculator outputs are general information only and should be verified against the current law, official guidance, and the specific facts of a matter.
